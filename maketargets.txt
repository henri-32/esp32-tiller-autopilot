For every following component (*) ... 

- hardwaretest-compass 

are the following variants of make targets provided: 

- configure_* (configures the CMake project according to the preset *)
- build_* (builds the CMake project according to the preset *)
- flash_* (builds and flashes the CMake build of the preset *) 

if it's a hardwaretest it also provides: 

- monitor_* (builds, flashes the CMake build of the preset * and starts monitoring the serial port)
